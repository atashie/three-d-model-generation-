### Finding 1.2 — Condensing to "every locator string + summary" strips negative evidence, counterexamples, and table context that justify `hypothesis` / `untested` status
* **Severity:** High
* **Design section & repo `file:line`:**
  * Design §6 ([2026-11-16-restructure-design.md](file:///C:/Users/arikt/documents/github/three-d-model-generation-/docs/plans/2026-11-16-restructure-design.md#L140-L152))
  * [CLAUDE.md](file:///C:/Users/arikt/documents/github/three-d-model-generation-/CLAUDE.md#L7-L11) (rules 2–4)
  * [perception.yaml](file:///C:/Users/arikt/documents/github/three-d-model-generation-/rules/perception.yaml#L8-L32)
  * [color.yaml](file:///C:/Users/arikt/documents/github/three-d-model-generation-/rules/color.yaml#L25-L145), [color.yaml](file:///C:/Users/arikt/documents/github/three-d-model-generation-/rules/color.yaml#L166-L174)
  * [geometry-flat.yaml](file:///C:/Users/arikt/documents/github/three-d-model-generation-/rules/geometry-flat.yaml#L54-L66)
  * [test_rules.py](file:///C:/Users/arikt/documents/github/three-d-model-generation-/tests/test_rules.py#L46-L52)
* **Concrete failure scenario:**
  `tests/test_rules.py` only checks `assert e.locator in text` (substring presence). Many rules in `rules/*.yaml` rely on surrounding rows, negative controls, or trade-off tables around the locator to prove why a rule is `provenance: hypothesis, validation: untested` rather than `measured`:
  * In [color.yaml](file:///C:/Users/arikt/documents/github/three-d-model-generation-/rules/color.yaml#L115-L124) (`color.outline.keep_area_width_ratio`), the locator is only Roadie's row (`"roadie: area=629004 (40.52% of mask), width p50=20.00 px, p90=72.20 px, max=116.16 px"`), while the `claim` explicitly depends on the adjacent un-cited rows: *"the next rows of the same table give Oddie's numbers"*.
  * In [perception.yaml](file:///C:/Users/arikt/documents/github/three-d-model-generation-/rules/perception.yaml#L9-L15) (`perception.alpha.min_opaque`), the locator is `"cutoffs 1, 64, 128, 192, 254 all give the exact same mask"`, and the surrounding table shows that the image has zero semi-transparent pixels—proving insensitivity rather than validation of `128`.
  * In [color.yaml](file:///C:/Users/arikt/documents/github/three-d-model-generation-/rules/color.yaml#L35-L45) (`color.extract.default_count`), the locator cites the `K=6` row, while the `K=5` and `K=7..8` rows in the same table show why `K=6` splits Roadie's outline unless shade-merging runs (`Decision 2`).
  * In [color.yaml](file:///C:/Users/arikt/documents/github/three-d-model-generation-/rules/color.yaml#L173), `calibration.test` contains an unchecked free-text citation to `"research/verification/2026-11-14-test-strips/ section 7"`, which `test_rules.py` does not inspect and which will become a dangling path once `research/verification/` is deleted.
  If a condenser preserves only the literal `locator` strings plus a prose summary, `test_rules.py` still passes while the negative controls and counter-rows that prevent false promotion from `hypothesis`/`untested` to `measured` are lost from `HEAD`.
* **Suggested change:**
  Require `docs/evidence/<topic>.md` to preserve complete measurement tables (all tested parameter rows, all tested images, and negative controls) verbatim rather than prose summaries around isolated locator lines, and audit all free-text paths in `rules/*.yaml` (such as `calibration.test` in [color.yaml](file:///C:/Users/arikt/documents/github/three-d-model-generation-/rules/color.yaml#L173) and comments in `rules/*.yaml`).

### Finding 1.3 — The `pre-restructure` git tag is not in-tree evidence and is absent in CI clones
* **Severity:** Medium
* **Design section & repo `file:line`:**
  * Design §6 ([2026-11-16-restructure-design.md](file:///C:/Users/arikt/documents/github/three-d-model-generation-/docs/plans/2026-11-16-restructure-design.md#L140-L152))
  * [ci.yml](file:///C:/Users/arikt/documents/github/three-d-model-generation-/.github/workflows/ci.yml#L17)
* **Concrete failure scenario:**
  Design §6 relies on a git tag (`pre-restructure`) as the fallback record for deleted scripts, reviews, and full logs, and rewrites [CLAUDE.md](file:///C:/Users/arikt/documents/github/three-d-model-generation-/CLAUDE.md#L11) rule 6 so future jobs and features also condense and delete their scripts/runs once done (without even specifying tags for future jobs). In [ci.yml](file:///C:/Users/arikt/documents/github/three-d-model-generation-/.github/workflows/ci.yml#L17), `actions/checkout@v4` performs a shallow (`fetch-depth: 1`) checkout without tags, and local worktrees or tarball downloads do not guarantee tags are fetched. Worse, `measure_*.py` scripts in `research/verification/` (e.g. `measure_palette.py`, `measure_conditionals.py`, `measure_coverage.py`) are the *only* record of how non-tdmg measurements were computed; deleting them means a future rule re-evaluation on a new image cannot re-run the measurement script on `HEAD`.
* **Suggested change:**
  Keep reusable measurement scripts (or a single `scripts/measure_*.py` / `docs/evidence/` appendix) in-tree whenever a `hypothesis` rule still awaits calibration or re-measurement on future prints, and require in-tree `docs/evidence/<topic>.md` to be self-contained so no rule in `rules/*.yaml` depends on git history to verify its claim.

### Finding 2.1 — `finish` only supports `model.3mf`, breaking R1 single-color (`model.stl`) and R2 glue-route (`glue-*.stl`) prints
* **Severity:** High
* **Design section & repo `file:line`:**
  * Design §1 ([2026-11-16-restructure-design.md](file:///C:/Users/arikt/documents/github/three-d-model-generation-/docs/plans/2026-11-16-restructure-design.md#L71)) & §4 ([2026-11-16-restructure-design.md](file:///C:/Users/arikt/documents/github/three-d-model-generation-/docs/plans/2026-11-16-restructure-design.md#L115-L124))
  * [flat.py](file:///C:/Users/arikt/documents/github/three-d-model-generation-/src/tdmg/flat.py#L46-L49), [colored_flat.py](file:///C:/Users/arikt/documents/github/three-d-model-generation-/src/tdmg/colored_flat.py#L525-L553), [color_slicing.py](file:///C:/Users/arikt/documents/github/three-d-model-generation-/src/tdmg/color_slicing.py#L194-L212)
* **Concrete failure scenario:**
  Design §4 specifies that `prints/<name>/` contains `model.3mf` (and `palette.json` for multicolor) and that `tdmg finish` checks "`model.3mf`'s hash equals the one that was sliced and judged".
  1. **R1 (single-color flat icon)** never produces a `model.3mf`; [flat.py](file:///C:/Users/arikt/documents/github/three-d-model-generation-/src/tdmg/flat.py#L47) writes `model.stl` and [slicing.py](file:///C:/Users/arikt/documents/github/three-d-model-generation-/src/tdmg/slicing.py#L318-L355) slices `model.stl`. An R1 job cannot be finished under §4.
  2. **R2 AMS multicolor** ([color_slicing.py](file:///C:/Users/arikt/documents/github/three-d-model-generation-/src/tdmg/color_slicing.py#L510-L526)) does **not** slice `model.3mf`! OrcaSlicer puts all shells of `model.3mf` on filament 1 ([orca_input.py](file:///C:/Users/arikt/documents/github/three-d-model-generation-/src/tdmg/orca_input.py#L4-L10)), so `color_slicing.py` builds and slices a separate `orca-input.3mf` per face (`slice/ams/face-down/orca-input.3mf` and `slice/ams/face-up/orca-input.3mf`), where `face-up` even rotates the meshes 180° about Y (`turn_over`). Thus `model.3mf`'s SHA-256 *never* equals the file that OrcaSlicer sliced; `slice.json` links `model.3mf` via `colored.json`'s `outputs["model.3mf"].sha256`.
  3. **R2 glue route** prints `glue-base.stl` and `glue-color-*.stl`, not `model.3mf`.
* **Suggested change:**
  Define `prints/<name>/` outputs per recipe/route: `model.stl` for R1, `model.3mf` + `palette.json` for R2 AMS, and `glue-base.stl` + `glue-color-*.stl` + `palette.json` for R2 glue. Specify that `finish` verifies the chain of hashes (`slice.json` `links` -> `colored.json` / `flat.json` `outputs`) rather than claiming `model.3mf` was directly sliced.

* **Design section & repo `file:line`:**

* **Concrete failure scenario:**
  When `select` is run with `--mask FILE` (`method="user_mask"` in [selection.py](file:///C:/Users/arikt/documents/github/three-d-model-generation-/src/tdmg/selection.py#L328-L355)), the mask cannot be derived from `source/<image>` and `job.yaml` settings alone—it comes from an external binary mask PNG. ### Finding 2.4 — Confirmation gate and path/reparse-point checks on Windows have bypass holes
* **Severity:** Medium
* **Design section & repo `file:line`:**
  * Design §3 ([2026-11-16-restructure-design.md](file:///C:/Users/arikt/documents/github/three-d-model-generation-/docs/plans/2026-11-16-restructure-design.md#L112-L113)), §4 ([2026-11-16-restructure-design.md](file:///C:/Users/arikt/documents/github/three-d-model-generation-/docs/plans/2026-11-16-restructure-design.md#L120-L124)), §5 ([2026-11-16-restructure-design.md](file:///C:/Users/arikt/documents/github/three-d-model-generation-/docs/plans/2026-11-16-restructure-design.md#L130-L137))
  * [records.py](file:///C:/Users/arikt/documents/github/three-d-model-generation-/src/tdmg/records.py#L77-L113)
* **Concrete failure scenario:**
  1. **Confirmation forgery / stale confirmation:** Design §3 says *"That confirmation is recorded in `job.yaml`. Claude never records one the owner did not give."* If `job.yaml` just has a static field (e.g. `confirmed: "looks good"`) and the owner re-runs a stage with tweaked parameters after confirming an earlier run, the confirmation in `job.yaml` is now stale for the new `model.3mf`, yet `finish` only checks that "`job.yaml` holds the owner's confirmation". Furthermore, nothing in code prevents Claude from editing `job.yaml` directly or running `finish` in the same turn as `run` unless confirmation binds to the final `slice_id` / `model_sha256` and timestamp.
  2. **Windows junctions & traversal in `NAME`:** On Windows (the target platform), `Path.is_symlink()` is `False` for NTFS directory junctions (`Path.is_junction()` in Python 3.12+ is required), and if `NAME` or `--print-name` is not validated with `_plain()` ([records.py](file:///C:/Users/arikt/documents/github/three-d-model-generation-/src/tdmg/records.py#L77-L79)), `jobs/../artifacts/tools` resolves outside `jobs/`.
* **Suggested change:**
  Require `confirmation` in `job.yaml` to bind to the exact `slice_id` and `model_sha256` being finished (so any re-run invalidates prior confirmation); validate both `NAME` and `--print-name` with `_plain()` (rejecting `.`, `..`, `/`, `\`, `:`); and walk `jobs/<name>` checking both `is_symlink()` and `is_junction()` before deletion.

  * [colored_flat.py](file:///C:/Users/arikt/documents/github/three-d-model-generation-/src/tdmg/colored_flat.py#L316-L335)

  1. `verify_colors(colors_dir, selection_dir)` ([colors_verify.py](file:///C:/Users/arikt/documents/github/three-d-model-generation-/src/tdmg/colors_verify.py#L112-L121)) requires `colors.json`, `labels.png`, `regions.png`, `selection.json`, `mask.png`, and `cutout.png`. It does **not** record the rule registry values (`rules/*.yaml` snapshot) or environment (`python`, `numpy`, `scipy`, `opencv-python-headless`, `shapely`, `trimesh`) that `selection.json`, `colors.json`, `colored.json`, and `slice.json` record. * **Suggested change:**
  Include the resolved `rules` snapshot (every rule id and value used by the job), the `slicer` pin hashes, the stage output SHA-256s (`mask.png`, `cutout.png`, `labels.png`, `model.3mf`), and the `environment` versions inside `prints/<name>/job.yaml` (or a single condensed `record.json` alongside `card.md`), so a finished print pins every parameter that produced its bytes even when `rules/*.yaml` defaults evolve later.

  Rewritten rule 6 says: *"Keep evidence, purge intermediates. Rules and pins cite `docs/evidence/*.md`; finished prints keep `card.md`, `job.yaml` and the source image in `prints/<name>/`. Everything produced along the way (job folders, scratch scripts, exploratory runs) is condensed into those records and deleted once the job or feature is done."*
  Meanwhile, Rule 3 says *"Keep kind, provenance, and validation separate"* (`hypothesis` + `untested` until calibrated on real prints) and Rule 4 says *"Never call something verified unless it was run."* If a feature's measurement scripts and exploratory runs are deleted *"once the feature is done"*, but the feature's rules are still `provenance: hypothesis, validation: untested` awaiting physical calibration prints (as R2's rules are right now!), deleting the measurement scripts and raw data before physical calibration completes destroys the scripts needed to re-evaluate the rule when calibration data arrives.
* **Suggested change:**
  Distinguish **print jobs** (`jobs/<name>/`, purged on `tdmg finish` once the owner confirms the physical print) from **rule/pin evidence** (whose measurement scripts and full tables must remain in `docs/evidence/` until the rule is calibrated and `validation: calibrated` / `provenance: measured`).

## 7. Missing Pieces and Over-Design (YAGNI)

### Finding 7.1 — Missing: Text-to-image intake, multiple color variants per subject, and calibration strip workflow
* **Severity:** Medium
* **Design section & repo `file:line`:**
  * Design Goal & §1–§4 ([2026-11-16-restructure-design.md](file:///C:/Users/arikt/documents/github/three-d-model-generation-/docs/plans/2026-11-16-restructure-design.md#L10-L128))
  * [STATUS.md](file:///C:/Users/arikt/documents/github/three-d-model-generation-/docs/STATUS.md#L131-L137), [PLAN.md](file:///C:/Users/arikt/documents/github/three-d-model-generation-/docs/PLAN.md#L97-L98)
* **Concrete failure scenario:**
  1. **Text-to-image provenance:** The owner's goal states *"Takes an image (either provided by the user, or generated from text through AI)"*. When an image is generated from a text prompt (e.g. via Claude Code or an image generator), `job.yaml` and `card.md` need fields for the generation prompt, generator/model id, and seed/parameters, not just a file path and SHA-256. Moreover, if the AI-generated image is written directly into `jobs/<name>/` and `jobs/<name>/` is cleaned or abandoned before `finish`, the generated source image is deleted.
  2. **Calibration strips (`tdmg strip`):** [STATUS.md](file:///C:/Users/arikt/documents/github/three-d-model-generation-/docs/STATUS.md#L131-L137) shows `tdmg strip` (`artifacts/strip-runs/2026-11-14/`) is already built and awaiting the owner's physical print results (`results-template.md`). Design §1–§7 never mentions where `tdmg strip` writes its output once `artifacts/` only holds `artifacts/tools/`, or where completed strip results live.
* **Suggested change:**
  Add optional `source_generation` metadata (`prompt`, `generator`, `created_at`) to `job.yaml`, store new source images in a persistent git-ignored intake folder (or `prints/<name>/source/` immediately) so `jobs/<name>/` never holds the sole copy of an image, and specify where `tdmg strip` outputs live (e.g. `jobs/strip-<date>/` or `artifacts/strip-runs/`).

### Finding 7.2 — Over-design (YAGNI): A 4-subcommand `tdmg job` state machine on top of existing stage commands for a single owner using Claude Code
* **Severity:** Low
* **Design section & repo `file:line`:**
  * Design §3 ([2026-11-16-restructure-design.md](file:///C:/Users/arikt/documents/github/three-d-model-generation-/docs/plans/2026-11-16-restructure-design.md#L103-L113)) & §7 Phase 2–3 ([2026-11-16-restructure-design.md](file:///C:/Users/arikt/documents/github/three-d-model-generation-/docs/plans/2026-11-16-restructure-design.md#L163-L169))
  * [cli.py](file:///C:/Users/arikt/documents/github/three-d-model-generation-/src/tdmg/cli.py#L1-L450)
* **Concrete failure scenario:**
  The owner is a single user driving `tdmg` through Claude Code (`make-print` skill). Adding four new subcommands (`tdmg job new`, `tdmg job run`, `tdmg job status`, `tdmg finish`) *while also keeping* `tdmg select`, `tdmg colors`, `tdmg flat`, `tdmg slice`, and `tdmg strip` means maintaining two parallel CLI layers with duplicated option parsing (`--size-mm`, `--thickness-mm`, `--colors`, `--merge`, `--outline`, `--face`, `--orcaslicer-dir`, `--prusaslicer-dir`). Worse, `tdmg job run NAME [--from STAGE]` has to re-parse all stage flags or require editing `job.yaml` by hand between stages.
* **Suggested change:**
  Keep `job.yaml` as the declarative job spec in `jobs/<name>/job.yaml`, and have just two job commands: `tdmg run jobs/<name>/job.yaml` (idempotently runs any stage whose inputs/settings in `job.yaml` changed and prints the status summary) and `tdmg finish jobs/<name>/job.yaml` (verifies hashes + owner confirmation, writes `prints/<name>/`, and deletes `jobs/<name>/`).
