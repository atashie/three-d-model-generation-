- **major — [Image to printable 3D model landscape.md:122](<../../../reports/Image to printable 3D model landscape.md#L122>) — UNSUPPORTED.** Claim: “Self-intersection, component count, thickness and build-volume fit come after as warnings.” This conflates file conformance with print readiness. The [3MF specification](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/3MFConsortium/spec_core/master/3MF%20Core%20Specification.md) permits consumers to process self-intersections; it does not justify accepting undersized walls or oversized parts. [Orca’s source](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/OrcaSlicer/OrcaSlicer/v2.5.0/src/OrcaSlicer.cpp) explicitly rejects plates without printable objects fully inside the print volume. **Fix:** make build-volume fit and calibrated minimum dimensions acceptance requirements. Keep self-intersection handling separate; the specification does not mandate a volumetric rebuild. **Hypothesis (a): the warning-tier problem is confirmed.**

- **major — [Image to printable 3D model landscape.md:147](<../../../reports/Image to printable 3D model landscape.md#L147>) — UNSUPPORTED.** Claim: “report the 5th percentile rather than the minimum.” The quoted cube experiment establishes disagreement between measurement methods, not that discarding the thinnest 5% is safe. A narrow connector can constitute less than 5% of samples and still determine whether the object survives printing. Face-count percentiles also change with tessellation. **Fix:** retain percentiles as summaries, but report localized thin regions, sampling coverage and measurement failures; validate critical features separately.

- **major — [Image to printable 3D model landscape.md:109](<../../../reports/Image to printable 3D model landscape.md#L109>) — VERIFIED-WRONG.** Claim: “the only texture-to-AMS conversion is commercial.” OrcaSlicer **v2.5.0** has `TextureImportDialog`, texture-color clustering and filament matching; `apply_textured_mesh_import_result()` calls `apply_painted_mesh_to_volume()` and assigns extruders. These are present in the [tagged source](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/OrcaSlicer/OrcaSlicer/v2.5.0/src/slic3r/GUI/Plater.cpp). **Fix:** document this local, open-source conversion path. Distinguish interactive filament painting from a tested headless converter and from generating separate watertight color shells. The narrower claim about a *permissively licensed* converter remains a separate question. **Hypothesis (c): confirmed.**

- **major — [Image to printable 3D model landscape.md:169](<../../../reports/Image to printable 3D model landscape.md#L169>) — MISSING.** Claim: “Its exit codes separate an empty plate (−50), a validation error (−51) … (−59).” Those are [internal constants](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/SoftFever/OrcaSlicer/main/src/libslic3r/Utils.hpp), not the normal exit statuses a macOS subprocess observes. [Apple documents](https://developer.apple.com/library/archive/documentation/System/Conceptual/ManPages_iPhoneOS/man3/exit.3.html) that only the low eight bits reach the parent. A read-only process probe here produced **−50 → 206, −51 → 205, −59 → 197**. **Fix:** document both representations and compare subprocess results against the positive values; distinguish signal termination separately. Also describe −50 as “no suitable printable objects,” which includes more than an empty plate. **Hypothesis (e): confirmed.**

- **major — [Image to printable 3D model landscape.md:283](<../../../reports/Image to printable 3D model landscape.md#L283>) — VERIFIED-WRONG.** Claims: “rembg requires <3.14,” “pymeshfix’s macOS arm64 wheels stop at 3.12,” and therefore text-to-cad “cannot share an interpreter with the perception and repair stack.” [rembg’s metadata](https://pypi.org/project/rembg/) permits `>=3.11,<4.0`; [pymeshfix](https://pypi.org/project/pymeshfix/) supplies `cp312-abi3` arm64 wheels. The erratum acknowledges both errors without correcting this reasoning in the body. **Fix:** update the body and compatibility table; justify any separate environment using actual remaining conflicts, such as selected `bpy` or CGAL versions. **Hypothesis (f): confirmed.**

- **major — [Image to printable 3D model landscape.md:216](<../../../reports/Image to printable 3D model landscape.md#L216>) — VERIFIED-WRONG.** Claim: “BOSL2’s print-in-place hinge defaults to a 0.2 mm gap plus a per-printer `$slop` value.” [The implementation](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/BelfrySCAD/BOSL2/master/hinges.scad) distinguishes mechanisms: `knuckle_hinge()` defaults to `gap=0.2`; `living_hinge_mask()` computes `default(hingegap, layerheight)+2*get_slop()`. Pin-hole enlargement is another separate use of `$slop`. **Fix:** name the mechanism and parameter explicitly, and provide its actual formula; do not combine these into one clearance rule. **Hypothesis (d): confirmed.**

- **minor — [Image to printable 3D model landscape.md:172](<../../../reports/Image to printable 3D model landscape.md#L172>) — VERIFIED-WRONG.** Claim: “No maintained Python wrapper for any slicer exists on PyPI.” The cited-notes candidate [fabprint](https://pypi.org/project/fabprint/) now redirects users to **estampo**. Its [current PyPI documentation](https://pypi.org/project/estampo/) explicitly describes wrapping OrcaSlicer and CuraEngine, with active development and a local-install fallback. **Fix:** list existing wrappers and their maturity/platform limitations; distinguish subprocess wrappers from in-process Python bindings. **Hypothesis (b): confirmed.**

- **minor — [Image to printable 3D model landscape.md:345](<../../../reports/Image to printable 3D model landscape.md#L345>) — VERIFIED-WRONG.** Erratum claim: stable-ABI wheels “install and run on 3.12 and every later version.” [pymeshfix 0.18.1](https://pypi.org/project/pymeshfix/) explicitly requires **Python <3.15**. ABI compatibility neither overrides package metadata nor proves runtime correctness. The [local verification record:46](<../../../research/verification/2026-11-08-stack-smoke/README.md#L46>) correctly says, “We tested 3.13 only.” **Fix:** state ABI compatibility, declared supported versions and actually tested versions separately.

- **minor — [Image to printable 3D model landscape.md:268](<../../../reports/Image to printable 3D model landscape.md#L268>) — OVERSTATED.** Claim: text-to-cad “does not accept images, and does not generate meshes.” Its [export documentation](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/earthtojake/text-to-cad/main/skills/cad/references/exports.md) explicitly describes generated meshes and STL/3MF/GLB export. Its [modeling instructions](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/earthtojake/text-to-cad/main/skills/cad/SKILL.md) also address interpreting images and drawings. That does not establish a dedicated image-reconstruction API, but the categorical wording is misleading. **Fix:** describe it as agent-driven parametric CAD with mesh export, rather than a single-image neural reconstruction model.

- **minor — [Image to printable 3D model landscape.md:3](<../../../reports/Image to printable 3D model landscape.md#L3>) — OVERSTATED.** Claim: “No generator documents watertight solid output.” The report itself later identifies Step1X-3D, whose [primary README](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/stepfun-ai/Step1X-3D/main/README.md) describes producing watertight TSDF representations. This is not proof that every exported mesh is printable, but it defeats the blanket documentation claim. **Fix:** say that no independently demonstrated guarantee of repair-free, FDM-ready output was established.

- **minor — [Image to printable 3D model landscape.md:174](<../../../reports/Image to printable 3D model landscape.md#L174>) — MISSING.** Recommendation: “seed its random samplers and assert byte-identical exports across runs.” The export recipe omits known nondeterministic identifiers: [trimesh’s exporter](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mikedh/trimesh/main/trimesh/exchange/threemf.py) calls `uuid.uuid4()`, and the [local verification record:84](<../../../research/verification/2026-11-08-stack-smoke/README.md#L84>) records random lib3mf UUIDs and the successful explicit-UUID workaround. **Fix:** specify stable object/instance identities and canonical archive metadata, then test geometric equivalence separately from byte identity.

**Things I checked and found correct**
